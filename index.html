Light Sensing and Control of an Agrovoltaic Plant

University of Peradeniya – EngEx 2025

What are Agrovoltaics?

Sri Lanka’s first semi-transparent agrovoltaics site (Kandy, Feb 2024). 85 kWp system over 800 m² tea estate; producing >100 MWh/year, with a 24 kWh battery powering 19 homes. Semi-transparent panels (39% light, 33% PAR) provide shade + energy. LED grow lights (red/blue) boost tea growth and reduce stress. Tea breeds: TRI 4006 (high yield, low blight tolerance) & TRI 4042 (moderate yield, better blight tolerance).

System Components

  • Light Controller: Grow light control to mitigate excessive shading.
  • IP Cameras: Continuous imaging of tea plants.
  • Sensor Modules: Irradiance, air temp, humidity, soil moisture, soil temp, soil EC.
  • Cloud Upload: Automatic transfer of images & sensor data for continuous monitoring and analysis.

AgroVision Monitoring System

Includes advanced image segmentation and continuous monitoring of crop conditions. Enables optimized decision making for both energy and agricultural output.
